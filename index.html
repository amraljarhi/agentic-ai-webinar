Move from AI-assisted coding to AI-powered software delivery. Join GitHub and Eficode for a practical, live session on how leading teams build, evaluate, govern, and scale developer workflows powered by intelligent agents.

What you'll walk away with

  • Understand what Agentic AI looks like across the modern SDLC — beyond IDE-based coding assistants.
  • See how leading teams automate planning, development, testing, review, and delivery with AI.
  • Practical examples of integrating AI with repositories, issue trackers, and CI/CD using tools such as GitHub Actions.
  • How to evaluate, test, and govern AI agents to ensure quality and trust at scale.
  • Patterns for scaling AI adoption across engineering teams with the right controls and guardrails.
  • Opportunities to apply agentic workflows in your own organization to accelerate delivery.
